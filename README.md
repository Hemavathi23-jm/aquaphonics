# 🌱 Aquaponics – Sustainable Farming Using Fish and Plants

## 📖 Overview

Aquaponics is a sustainable farming technique that integrates aquaculture (raising fish) and hydroponics (growing plants without soil) into a single ecosystem. This project demonstrates how fish waste can be converted into nutrients for plants, while plants naturally filter and clean the water for fish.

The system creates a self-sustaining cycle that conserves water, reduces waste, and eliminates the need for chemical fertilizers.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Demonstrate the working principle of an aquaponics system.
- Promote sustainable and eco-friendly farming methods.
- Understand the nitrogen cycle and water recirculation process.
- Produce healthy plants and fish simultaneously.
- Reduce water consumption compared to traditional agriculture.

---

## 🔄 Working Principle

1. Fish produce waste containing ammonia.
2. Beneficial bacteria convert ammonia into nitrites and nitrates.
3. Plants absorb nitrates as nutrients for growth.
4. Plants filter the water naturally.
5. Clean water is returned to the fish tank.

This process continuously repeats, creating a balanced ecosystem.

## 📊 Advantages

- Uses up to 90% less water than traditional farming.
- No chemical fertilizers required.
- Produces fish and vegetables together.
- Environmentally friendly.
- Suitable for urban and indoor farming.
- Reduces water pollution and waste.
